# The Designer's Constitution

Static source of the essay published at https://constitution.design/.

- Plain HTML, CSS and classic scripts with relative paths.
  `index.html` holds the English text (served at `/`). `es/index.html` is the
  Spanish page (served at `/es`), generated from `index.html` by
  `node constitution/tools/build-es.mjs`, which also holds the Spanish copy;
  `--check` fails when it is out of date. Edit `index.html` or the tool, never
  the generated file.
- `assets/js/i18n.js` exposes the page language and the wheel labels, and
  stores an explicit EN/ES/PT choice in the `pz-lang` cookie.
- The parent site serves a copy of this folder from `public/lab/constitution/`
  and maps the `constitution.design` host to it.
- Fonts are not part of this public repository. Timeless Text and Timeless
  Grotesk (Timeless Free Font License 1.2, which does not allow public
  redistribution) live only in the parent site's copy under
  `assets/fonts/`. The PabloZarate™ wordmark font is loaded from
  pablozarate.com.
- `assets/js/analytics.js` only runs on the production origin.

- Ring geometry lives in `assets/models/blender-rings.js`; `assets/optics/` contains five baked studio views per layer. The runtime keeps scroll geometry and camera motion, composes translucent glass, and applies a subtle coral hover to individual fragments. Reflections are precalculated, not live ray tracing. Approved EN/ES/PT renders are the fallback.

## Brazilian Portuguese

- `pt/index.html` at `/pt` is generated by `node constitution/tools/build-pt.mjs`
  from the original English structure and `tools/pt-BR.json`; `--check` verifies
  reproducibility and complete marker coverage. Rebuild Spanish and Portuguese
  whenever the English structure changes.
- The initial Portuguese text was contributed through
  https://constitutiondesign.vercel.app/ (reviewed 2026-10-20). The published
  page did not identify its translator or declare a reuse licence. The project
  owner requested this integration; no third-party code, images, fonts or
  accessibility scripts were imported. The Portuguese footer links to the
  community contribution without inventing a translator identity or licence.
- Review against all 106 English text blocks retained Brazilian Portuguese and
  corrected the material-breaking metaphor, “o que está em jogo”, Vignelli’s
  original “design is one”, the technical terms “prompts” and “skills”, the
  specific reference to “vibe coding”, and the closing emphasis on shipping.
  Pablo Zárate’s authorship and the historical Gropius credit remain intact.
- The rings model capabilities, not audience data. All five layers, six studies,
  seven materials, three human responsibilities and four building questions
  retain their existing meaning and geometry. `assets/js/i18n.js` supplies the
  Portuguese labels. The Portuguese fallback and social card are rendered from
  this project's own page and assets, not copied from the contribution.
